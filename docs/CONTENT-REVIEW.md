## 0. Release blockers

### Missing privacy policy — blocks production candidate-data collection

Recorded per explicit instruction, SEO audit Phase 3 Batch 4 corrective pass. **No privacy policy, cookie notice or terms page exists anywhere on this site** — no such route is approved in `docs/MASTER-SITEMAP.md`, so none has been built (see section 3 below, first flagged in the Phase 3 content pass).

This is a **release blocker**, not a content-quality note: `/for-candidates/` and `/talent-pool/` actively invite candidates to submit personal information (CV, contact details, skills/experience), and `/contact/`, `/find-talent/` invite employer contact details, with no linked notice explaining how that information is handled. The short "handled responsibly and only shared with your agreement" sentence on `/for-candidates/` is a plain-English summary for the visitor, **not a substitute for a proper privacy notice** — it does not state a controller, a lawful basis, retention periods or individual rights, and must not be treated as satisfying that requirement.

**Before any production candidate-data collection goes live**, a business-approved, UK-appropriate privacy notice is required, drafted or reviewed by a qualified party (not generated by an AI coding agent). It will eventually need to explain, at minimum:

- Who controls the data (the data controller).
- What candidate and employer information is collected.
- Why it is processed (the purposes).
- The lawful basis for each purpose (UK GDPR).
- Who information may be shared with (e.g. employers a candidate is introduced to, any processors).
- How long information is retained.
- Individual rights (access, rectification, erasure, objection, etc.) and how to exercise them.
- Contact details for privacy queries (and, if applicable, a Data Protection Officer).
- Cookies and analytics, once GA4/GSC or similar are wired up (see section 7).

This list is scope for a future legal/business review — it is not permission to draft or publish policy wording in any batch, and no such wording has been written.
